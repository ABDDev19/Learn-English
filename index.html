ABOUT ENGLISH BRIDGE

A friendly learning space for beginner English.

English Bridge is designed to make English practice simple, practical, and consistent. Students can study the lessons, build vocabulary with Pashto meanings, listen to stories and conversations, practice speaking, and test their knowledge.

📖

How to use the lessons

Start with Unit 1 and move forward one lesson at a time. Read the explanation, study the examples, then practice the topic in the quiz.

🗣️

How to practice speaking

Open a conversation, read each line aloud, listen to the voice, and repeat it. Practice both sides of the conversation with a partner when possible.

🎧

How to improve listening

Listen to a story or conversation more than once. First listen for the general meaning, then listen again and repeat the sentences you hear.

🧠

How to remember words

Learn a small group of words each day. Say the English word, check the Pashto meaning, read the example, and use the word in your own sentence.

⏱️

A simple daily routine

Try 10 minutes of vocabulary, 10 minutes of a lesson, and 10 minutes of speaking or listening. Regular short practice is easier to maintain than one long session.

👨‍🏫

For teachers

Use the conversations for pair work, the vocabulary cards for quick review, the stories for listening practice, and the quizzes for checking progress.
